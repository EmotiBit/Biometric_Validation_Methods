# sit-stand-sit_v0.0.1
Procedure for recording data under the environment of sitting, then standing, then sitting again

## Procedure
1) Remain still and seated for 3.5 minutes
2) Slowly stand up (about 5 seconds) 
3) Remain standing for 3.5 minutes
4) Slowly sit down (about 5 seconds)
5) Remain seated for 3.5 minutes.
