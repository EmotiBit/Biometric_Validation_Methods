# simulated-obstructed-airway_v0.0.0
Procedure for recording data under the environment of breathing against a simulated obstructed airway.

⚠️ **THIS PROCEDURE INVOLVES PURPOSEFUL APNEA. IF YOU START TO FEEL LIGHTHEADED OR SpO2 DROPS BELOW 80%, PLEASE DISCONTINUE THE PROCEDURE** ⚠️

## Procedure
When instructed to breathe in/out against a closed airway, pinch nose shut and keep lips together, similar to the [valsalva maneuver](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/23209-valsalva-maneuver). It is also recommended to keep eyes closed when breathing out.

⚠️ **THIS PROCEDURE INVOLVES PURPOSEFUL APNEA. IF YOU START TO FEEL LIGHTHEADED OR SpO2 DROPS BELOW 80%, PLEASE DISCONTINUE THE PROCEDURE** ⚠️

## Procedure
1) Sit with back straight
2) Breathe normally for 3 minutes
3) Breathe out, then breathe in against closed airway for 10 seconds
4) Breathe normally for 20 seconds
5) Repeat steps 3 and 4 for another 4 cycles
6) Breathe normally for 3 minutes
7) Breathe in, then breathe out against closed airway for 10 seconds
8) Breathe normally for 20 seconds
9) Repeat steps 7 and 8 for another 4 cycles
10) Breathe normally for 3 minutes
