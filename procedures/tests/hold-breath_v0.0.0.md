# hold-breath_v0.0.0
Procedure for recording data under the environment of apnea with an unobstructed airway (holding breath).

⚠️ **THIS PROCEDURE INVOLVES PURPOSEFUL APNEA. IF YOU START TO FEEL LIGHTHEADED OR SpO2 DROPS BELOW 90%, PLEASE DISCONTINUE THE PROCEDURE** ⚠️

## Procedure
1) Sit with back straight
2) Breathe normally for 3 minutes
3) Hold breath for 15 seconds after an inhale
4) Breathe normally for 1 minute
5) Hold breath for 30 seconds after an inhale
6) Breathe normally for 2 minutes
7) Hold breath for 45 seconds after an inhale
8) Breathe normally for 2 minutes
9) Hold breath for 1 minute after an inhale
10) Breathe normally for 3 minutes
